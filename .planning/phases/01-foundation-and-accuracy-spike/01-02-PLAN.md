## Phase Goal

**As a** team member, **I want to** open the app on a real phone and a laptop over HTTPS, enter my height, and see live body landmarks and a silhouette computed entirely on the device with first shoulder and chest readings, **so that** we can judge whether the scan is feasible.

<objective>
Set up pinned tooling after a human package check, self-host the body detection files with recorded hashes, and serve the app over HTTPS on the LAN without exposing the validation data.

Purpose: Everything later in the phase runs on these files. Exact pins and hashes make the build reproducible, self-hosting keeps frames on the device (PRIV-02), and HTTPS on the LAN is what lets a phone use its camera.
Output: `package.json` with exact pins, an HTTPS dev server that denies `validation/` and `.planning/`, `vendor/mediapipe/` with recorded hashes, and a test that checks the pins.

This plan runs in wave 1 beside plan 01-01 (removal of the old code and the page shell). The two plans share no file, and nothing here reads or tests `index.html`, `js/` or `assets/`.
</objective>
