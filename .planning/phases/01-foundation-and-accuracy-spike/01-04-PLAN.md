These settle the "Claude's Discretion" items that belong to this plan. They are fixed before any data exists.

1. Tape-to-size rule (D-12). The size is the smallest plain-tee size whose garment bust circumference (flat chest x 2) is at least the bust value plus `MIN_EASE_CM = 8`. With the plain tee (garment bust 94, 98, 102, 106, 110 cm) that gives:

   | Bust (cm) | Size |
   |---|---|
   | 82.0 or less | below-XS |
   | above 82.0 up to 86.0 | XS |
   | above 86.0 up to 90.0 | S |
   | above 90.0 up to 94.0 | M |
   | above 94.0 up to 98.0 | L |
   | above 98.0 up to 102.0 | XL |
   | above 102.0 | above-XL |

   The 8 cm figure is the lower edge of the retail "regular" ease band in research FEATURES.md section 5. It exists only so tape and scan are scored by one written rule. Phase 2's fit engine owns the real ease windows.
2. Borderline band: a value within `BORDERLINE_BAND_CM = 1.0` of a boundary (82, 86, 90, 94, 98, 102) is borderline, and its neighbour is the size on the other side of that boundary. A 2 cm band would flag every value because sizes are only 4 cm apart (D-07); 1.0 cm leaves the middle half of each size unflagged.
3. Agreement: `same` when tape and scan land on the same size; `adjacent-borderline` when they are one size apart, the scan value is borderline, and its neighbour is the tape size; otherwise `disagree`.
4. "Most subjects" means more than half. With two subjects that is both. With fewer than two the outcome is INSUFFICIENT DATA.
5. Reading log: numbers are read from the screen and typed into `validation/readings.csv`. There is no export button.
6. Tape bust is measured over a bra or thin fitted layer, not over the everyday top. D-11 (everyday clothes) applies to the scans. Confirmed by Jordan at plan approval (2026-10-03), so it is a user decision for this phase. Because the tape and the scan see different layers, every scan-versus-tape bust gap includes the everyday-clothing effect, and PASS-CRITERIA.md says so.
7. Scan bust row (user decision at plan approval, 2026-10-03, replacing the earlier fixed 28% level): for each view, the bust row is the widest torso row of the silhouette inside a band from `BUST_BAND_MIN_FRAC = 0.22` to `BUST_BAND_MAX_FRAC = 0.36` of the way from the shoulder-landmark midpoint down to the hip-landmark midpoint (implemented in plan 01-06). The torso width of a row is the contiguous mask run that contains the body midline (the straight line from the shoulder midpoint to the hip midpoint, which is the shoulder midpoint's column for an upright person); the front pose is an A-pose, so arms form separate runs and are excluded. Ties go to the higher row. Band justification (planner's estimate from adult female body proportions, not measured on the subjects): the shoulder-landmark-to-hip-landmark distance is roughly 28% of stature, and along it the armpit sits at about 0.16, the bust point at about 0.26 and the underbust at about 0.43. Starting at 0.22 leaves about 3 cm below the armpit so a sleeve or the arm-torso junction is not picked; ending at 0.36 covers bust points about 4 to 5 cm lower than average while staying about 3 cm above the underbust. Side view: the side mask uses its own widest (deepest) row in the same band, not the front row's fraction, because the deepest side row is where the fullest point of the bust shows, and the front and side captures are separate bursts, so matching rows would make the side reading depend on which capture came first. Both row fractions are logged. The constants are frozen before any reading, are not URL parameters, and are tuned only in Phase 3.
8. Shoulder PASS-WITH-OFFSET (user decision at plan approval, 2026-10-03): kept as a finding, because Phase 3 fits a calibration, but it does not meet D-13's 2.5 cm. It can produce at most a CONDITIONAL overall outcome, never GO, and Phase 3 must confirm the offset.
9. Reading notes carry the typed height (`h=`) and the front and side bust row fractions (`ff=`, `sf=`) in a fixed token format written in PROTOCOL.md, so the row choice can be judged later. No new CSV column.
10. Frozen set: PASS-CRITERIA.md, DEFINITIONS.md, PROTOCOL.md, `src/core/sizeFromTape.js`, the bust row selection in `src/core/mask.js` and the two bust band constants. Plan 01-07 records the freeze commit, and plans 01-07 and 01-08 diff all of them against it.
