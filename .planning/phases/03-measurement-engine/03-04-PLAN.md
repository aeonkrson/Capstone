## Phase Goal

**As a** shopper, **I want to** stand for a front and a side capture with my typed height as scale, **so that** I get calibrated shoulder, chest, waist, torso and arm measurements in cm, with bad captures caught before I trust the result.

Wire the engine (03-01), quality gates (03-02) and profile (03-03) into the existing scan page: a simple capture trigger per view, reasons on rejection, the five-value result with estimate markers, and a Use button that fills the profile.

Purpose: ROADMAP Phase 3 success criteria 1 and 3 become visible to the user; scan populates the fit check.
Output: gated burst, scan session, plain scan view, page wiring, i18n strings.
</objective>
