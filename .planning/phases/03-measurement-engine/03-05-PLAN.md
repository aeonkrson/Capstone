## Phase Goal

**As a** shopper, **I want to** stand for a front and a side capture with my typed height as scale, **so that** I get calibrated shoulder, chest, waist, torso and arm measurements in cm, with bad captures caught before I trust the result.

<objective>
Close ROADMAP Phase 3 success criteria 2 and 4: measure repeatability and tape accuracy on the team, fit the calibration from that data, and record any shortfall.

Purpose: "calibrated" must mean fitted against tape, and the ~1 cm repeatability claim must be measured, not assumed.
Output: definitions for the new measurements, readings file, calibration script with test, human scan session, fitted constants, results record.
</objective>
