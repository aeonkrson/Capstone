New English strings (key: text). Indonesian: natural Bahasa Indonesia in the same tone as existing id.js, same {params}:
- consent.title: Before the camera starts
- consent.use: The camera is used only to find your body outline and measure it. This happens on this device.
- consent.saved: No photo or video is saved or sent anywhere. Only the measurements you choose to save are kept, on this device.
- consent.sound: The scan gives sound cues, so turn your volume up. Stand back so your whole body is in view; the scan captures by itself when you stand still.
- consent.accept: I agree, start the camera
- consent.decline: Not now, I will type my measurements
- guide.front: Face the camera. Fit your whole body inside the outline, arms a little away from your sides.
- guide.side: Turn so your side faces the camera. Let your arms hang relaxed.
- guide.hold: Hold still…
- guide.capturing: Capturing, keep still…
- guide.turn: Front view captured. Now turn sideways.
- guide.done: Both views captured.
- guide.restart: Start the scan over
- guide.manual-mode: Manual capture is on. Use the Capture buttons below.
- manual.go: Enter measurements by hand
- manual.routed: The camera is not available. You can type your measurements here instead.
- manual.declined: The camera was not started. You can type your measurements here instead.
- scan.manual.note: If the automatic scan does not lock, you can capture each view with these buttons.
- review.title: Check your measurements
- review.intro: These come from your scan. Correct any value that looks wrong, then check the fit.
- review.name.label: Your name (to save the measurements)
- review.source.scanned: scanned
- review.source.edited: edited
- review.source.missing: not measured, type a value
- review.marker: ({source})
- review.marker-estimate: ({source}, estimate)
- review.save: Save and check the fit
- review.saved: Saved. Pick a size to check the fit.
